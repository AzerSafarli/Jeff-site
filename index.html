About Jeff Interactive

We believe that learning English should be more than studying grammar and memorizing vocabulary. Learning English should be a enjoyable and interactive experience. Learning English should feel like a conversation, not a classroom drill. Created by a Jeff Colleges student, this platform is designed to make speaking lessons more dynamic. Speaking lessons are powered by a variety of games and activities. From Taboo, Imposter and Hangman to Speaking Cards, every activity is designed to encourage communication, expand vocabulary, improve fluency and build confidence in speaking English.
